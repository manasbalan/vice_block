# VICE CITY BLOCKS: The Low-Spec GTA 6 (Roblox Edition)

A 3D blocky open-world crime game built for low-spec PCs, potato laptops, and browsers. Inspired by the neon sunset aesthetic of Leonida/Vice City, Roblox *Jailbreak*, and the Bonnie-and-Clyde duo storyline.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### Option 1: Quick Double-Click (Recommended)
1. Navigate to: `C:\Users\rames\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\vice-block-gta`
2. Double-click `play.bat` (or open `index.html` directly in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Firefox).
3. The game starts instantly at 60 FPS with zero lag!

### Option 2: Run via Terminal
```bash
python server.py
```
Open `http://localhost:8000` in your web browser.

## 🌴 Features & Highlights

* **Roblox R6 Blocky Aesthetic:** Classic blocky proportions, head bobbing, swinging limb animations, and seated driving poses.
* **Dual Protagonists:** Seamlessly switch between **Lucia** (pink outfit & bandana) and **Jason** (teal shirt & backward cap) anytime.
* **Full 5-Mission Storyline:**
  1. **Meet at the Ride:** Grab the Pink Cheetah sports coupe parked outside the Ocean Motel.
  2. **Gas & Go Stickup:** Walk into the gas station convenience store, punch the cash register, and grab $2,500 loot!
  3. **Lose the Heat:** Dodge the police cruiser pursuit and make it to the Safehouse Garage to stash the money.
  4. **The Big Bank Heist:** Hit the downtown Vice National Bank, drill the vault, and grab $15,000 in diamonds under 4-Star Wanted heat!
  5. **The Keys Getaway:** High-speed pursuit across the highway bridge to the ultimate safehouse mansion!
  6. **Free Roam Mode:** Unlimited open-world sandbox—steal police cars, hit stunt ramps, rob stores, and set high scores.
* **Police AI & Wanted Star System:** Police cruisers track you down with flashing lights, sirens, and ramming tactics. Break line of sight or visit the Safehouse to wipe your stars.
* **Zero Lag on Potato PCs:** Built using optimized Three.js WebGL with flat Lambert shading, zero heavy textures, and hardware acceleration that runs smoothly on Intel HD graphics.
* **100% Offline Procedural Audio:** Synthesized Miami Vice synthwave bassline and real-time audio effects (engine sounds, horn, police sirens, Roblox "OOF", and cash register chimes) using the Web Audio API—no missing sound files or internet connection required!
